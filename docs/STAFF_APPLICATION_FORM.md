# Vyntric Staff Application Form & Recruitment Guide
*Powered by Vyntric Automated Ecosystem*

---

## 1. Minimum Prerequisites
Before applying, applicants must meet the following criteria:
* **Age:** Minimum 15 years old (exceptions considered for exceptional maturity).
* **Communication:** Functional microphone and ability to communicate in voice channels.
* **Integrity:** Clean record with no history of malicious cheating, exploits, or blacklisting.
* **Activity:** Able to dedicate at least 12 to 15 hours per week to the server and community.

## 3. Management Evaluation Rubric

When reviewing submitted applications, management evaluates responses against the following standards:

| Criteria | Red Flags (Reject) | Green Flags (Interview) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Effort & Articulation** | Single-sentence dismissive answers, copy-pasted text | Thoughtful, well-structured, detailed explanations |
| **Scenario Judgment** | "I will ban immediately without proof" | "Record clear video proof, consult protocol, apply correct tier" |
| **Maturity & Humility** | Boasting about ownership titles, defensive attitude | Calm demeanor, eagerness to learn, team-first mentality |
| **Availability** | Unrealistic commitments or under 5h/week | Consistent, reliable daily schedule |

---

## 4. Onboarding Process
1. **Application Review:** Applications are evaluated by management within 72 hours.
2. **Voice Interview:** Shortlisted candidates are invited for a 10-15 minute voice interview.
3. **Trial Period:** Successful candidates enter a **14-day trial period** via `/trial add`.
4. **Promotion:** After positive review, trials are concluded with `/trial conclude` to full staff status.
