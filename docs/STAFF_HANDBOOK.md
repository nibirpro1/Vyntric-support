# Vyntric Staff Handbook & Operating Guidelines (SOP)
*Powered by Vyntric Automated Ecosystem*

---

## 1. Introduction & Mission
**Vyntric** is a premier competitive Minecraft gaming network (UHC, SMP, PvP, and custom ecosystem). Our staff team's primary mission is to ensure every player enjoys an orderly, fair, competitive, and cheat-free environment.

As a staff member, you represent the **Vyntric brand**. Every action, decision, and message reflects the integrity of the network.

---

## 2. Staff Code of Conduct

### 2.1 Zero Power Abuse
* Staff permissions and in-game commands (Creative mode, Teleport, Fly, Item Spawning, God mode) must never be used for personal gain, advantage, or favoring friends.
* Any staff member found abusing their authority will face immediate demotion and a potential blacklist from the network.

### 2.2 Professionalism & Composure
* Players may occasionally become heated, frustrated, or toxic. As a staff member, you must never lose your composure or retaliate.
* Always communicate politely, calmly, and objectively. Never engage in arguments in public chat channels. Direct unresolved issues to support tickets.

### 2.3 Strict Confidentiality
* Internal staff channels (`#staff-chat`, `#internal-task-board`, `#staff-announcements`), developer meetings, and upcoming update plans are strictly confidential.
* Leaking unreleased features, internal discussions, staff disagreements, or player personal data (IP addresses, Discord IDs, alt accounts) will result in an immediate permanent ban.

### 2.4 Absolute Impartiality
* Rules apply universally. If a friend, teammate, or known community member violates the rules, enforce the exact same punishment protocol. Favoritism is strictly prohibited.

---

## 3. Ticket & Support SOP

1. **Claiming Tickets:**
   * Claim an incoming ticket promptly to prevent multiple staff members from overlapping or causing confusion.
2. **Greeting Standard:**
   * Greet the player courteously:
     > *"Hello! Thank you for contacting Vyntric Support. How can we assist you today?"*
3. **Service Level Agreements (SLAs):**
   * **General Support:** Initial response within 15 to 30 minutes during active hours.
   * **Claim Reward:** Proof verified and prize delivered within 24 hours.
   * **Team Applications:** Evaluated and answered within 72 hours.
4. **Ticket Closure:**
   * Verify with the player that their issue is completely resolved before closing. Always provide a clear closing reason.

---

## 4. Punishment Escalation Ladder

| Level | Offense Category | Prescribed Action | Proof Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | Minor chat spam, excessive caps, light toxicity | Verbal warning or `/warn` | Optional (Log recommended) |
| **Tier 2** | Repeated toxicity, harassment, abusive language | 1 Hour to 24 Hour Mute | Screenshots required |
| **Tier 3** | Severe hate speech, unauthorized server advertising | 3 Day to 7 Day Mute / Temp Ban | Screenshots / Chat log required |
| **Tier 4** | Cheating/Hacks (KillAura, X-Ray, Fly, Reach, AutoClicker) | 30 Days to Permanent Ban | **Video proof 100% mandatory** |
| **Tier 5** | Doxxing, threats, malicious exploit/dupe abuse | Immediate Permanent Ban + Blacklist | Video / System logs required |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **NO PROOF, NO BAN Policy:** Never ban a player based on suspicion alone. Concrete, unedited evidence must be logged before or immediately after issuing a ban.

---

## 5. Proof Standards
1. **Video Requirements:**
   * Minimum resolution 720p at 30fps.
   * The target player's full in-game nametag must be clearly visible.
   * Upload video evidence to YouTube (Unlisted) or Medal.tv/Streamable.
2. **Logging via Bot:**
   * Immediately after issuing a punishment, log the evidence using the bot command:
     ```
     /proof add target:<PlayerIGN> type:ban evidence:<VideoURL> reason:<Reason>
     ```
   * This maintains an immutable audit trail for appeals.

---

## 6. Activity, Meetings & Leave of Absence (LOA)
* **Weekly Activity:** Maintain your agreed minimum active hours on the server and Discord.
* **Team Sync Meetings:** Attend regular team sync meetings. When announced via `/meeting`, confirm your attendance using the RSVP button.
* **Leave of Absence (LOA):** If you need time off due to exams, travel, or personal reasons, inform management at least 24 hours in advance.

---

## 7. Recognition & Staff of the Month
* At the conclusion of each month, exceptional dedication, consistency, and positive impact are recognized through the **Staff of the Month / MVP** award.
* Honorees receive custom Discord badges, server perks, and official recognition via `/staffmvp award`.
